Admission Conditions

Please read the following admission conditions carefully before enrolling at Success Center.

Code of Conduct

The academy reserves the right to dismiss any student who behaves inappropriately or violates ethical standards and public conduct.

Attendance Commitment

Students must adhere to lecture schedules and academy instructions. Absence of more than three sessions results in suspension, and continuation requires a fine of 15 JOD.

Financial Commitment

The payment receipt sent via email is considered a formal commitment by the student to pay all required fees.

No Refund Policy

Students are not entitled to request a refund of paid fees if they withdraw by personal choice. All outstanding balances must be settled before withdrawal.

Certificate Collection

Certificates are issued on dates determined by the academy after course completion. If not collected within two weeks, the certificate will be considered void.

Course Postponement

Students may postpone a course or diploma by paying a postponement fee of 10 JOD for language courses and 15 JOD for diplomas. Postponement ranges from one month up to twelve months.

Class Merging & Instructor Changes

The academy reserves the right to merge classes when applicable and change instructors at any level if necessary.

Repeating a Level

If a student fails an exam and repeats a level, a fee of 10 JOD will be charged.

Course Transfer

A fee of 10 JOD is applied if a student wishes to transfer from one course to another.

Course Interruption

If a student discontinues a course without official postponement, they are considered withdrawn. A fee of 25 JOD applies if they wish to resume the course.

Schedule Change

Students may change schedules only at the end of a level. Changes during an active level are not permitted.

Certificate Issuance Fee

The fee for issuing an academy certificate is 10 JOD.

Success Center

Success Center is your partner in growth, dedicated to helping you achieve your academic and professional goals through specialized support programs and services.
